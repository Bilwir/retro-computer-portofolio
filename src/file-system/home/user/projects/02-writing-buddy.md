



## *Terror Forsaken: Requiem Of The Gods*
## 2025-Present

### • World Building, Skript, Story Design
### • Paper, SkBee, Vault
A revival of Terror Legends' nostalgia for TC's loyal old members, Terror Forsaken became the best-selling private series and the most popular in Terror Community's history. It drew attention from larger communities like Brutal Entertainment and opened collaboration and partnership opportunities with Destruction Community, Omega Community, and Dipa Nusantara. I designed the world lore, race conflicts, and story arcs, and built a custom Gold & Black admin panel in Skript with live server-health monitoring.
